As of today, [Flathub Discourse][discourse] is read-only. Existing threads stay
available, but it is no longer possible to start new topics or reply.

<!-- truncate -->

We opened the forum [back in 2020][forum] as a place for asynchronous
communication that isn't GitHub issues or IRC. Realistically, it never took
off, becoming another issue tracker to keep an eye on. A forum where people
post and wait for an answer that never comes is worse than no forum at all.

Here's where to go instead:

- **Help with packaging and submissions**: the [Flathub Matrix
  channel][matrix-flathub], or the [Flatpak channel][matrix-flatpak] for
  questions about Flatpak itself.
- **Problems with an app**: the issue tracker of its repository, linked as
  "Manifest" on the app's Flathub page. Issues with Flathub itself go to
  [flathub/flathub][issues].
- **App requests**: Flathub doesn't take them. Ask the app's developers to
  [submit it][submission], or do it yourself.
- **Runtime announcements**: runtime projects such as freedesktop-sdk are
  welcome to post them on this blog.

Thanks to everyone who answered questions there over the years.
